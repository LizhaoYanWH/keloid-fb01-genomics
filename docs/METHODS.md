# Analysis methods


### S1. Analysis units and RNA state definitions

The genetic and RNA resources were not linked at the individual level. GSE220300 and GSE335482 each contributed four documented lesion patients; captures from the same patient were pooled for patient descriptions. Additional NS01/MS-coded captures in GSE220300 remained in whole-cohort scoring but did not increase the patient denominator. GSE335482 retained original GEO region labels, including the P2 source-label discrepancy. D0 comprised three unwounded donors. These resources supplied genetic, regulatory and cellular contexts rather than individual genotypes or patient-risk estimates.

Final lineage crosswalks identified 26,498 and 6,774 fibroblasts in the two discovery cohorts. The preceding lineage audit required reference probability ≥0.9, at least three canonical markers and a higher competing-lineage marker score for reassignment. Ambiguous profiles were retained. Frozen states used 3,000 capture-aware variable genes, 40 PCs, patient-only Harmony, 20 neighbors over 30 components, Leiden resolution 0.4 and seed 20261020 [@Korsunsky2019fast]. Each cohort retained its own labels. Cross-study correspondence used top-50 non-housekeeping marker Jaccard overlap and common-gene centroid Spearman correlation. Genetic scores did not select the clustering or state correspondence.

### S2. Genetic gene set and colocalization

The complete GRCh37 European GCST90652488 input was harmonized to the European 1000 Genomes reference without association-strength filtering. MAGMA v1.10 used NCBI37.3 gene locations, 10-kb flanks, SNP-wise mean association, total N=1,282,582 and seed 20261020 [@deLeeuw2015MAGMA; @Greene2025keloids]. The 19,427-gene ledger retained unavailable entries. Official scDRS conversion selected the top 1,000 genes with Z weights; absent RNA members were not replaced.

For colocalization, the 27 published signals defined 22 merged ±1-Mb windows. Complete nominal QTLs from cultured fibroblasts and non-sun-exposed/sun-exposed skin were harmonized by variant identity and alleles. Identity-confirmed variants with unresolved signed direction could contribute to unsigned ABFs but not to directional interpretation. Official coloc 6.0.3 functions used GWAS beta/SE² and case-control type, and QTL beta/SE², MAF, context sample size and quantitative type, with sdY estimated by the official procedure. All 2,391 units were evaluated at p1=p2=10⁻⁴ and p12=10⁻⁶, 10⁻⁵ or 10⁻⁴; the middle setting was primary. PP0–PP4, effective priors, warnings and NA were retained. PP4 was interpreted under the single-causal-variant assumption, not as FDR. Overlapping GTEx donor origins and sparse shared-variant coverage constrain interpretation [@GTEx2020atlas; @Giambartolomei2014coloc].

### S3. Whole-cohort scDRS and patient summaries

Original integer counts and exact cell identities were retained. Approved one-to-one Ensembl/Entrez mappings and detection in at least 50 cells determined gene admission. Full-library log1p UMI and detected-gene covariates were computed before filtering. Counts were normalized to 10,000 per cell and log1p-transformed. Official scDRS v1.0.4 used mean–variance matching, 20 mean and variance bins, `adj_prop=None`, variance-stabilizing weights, 1,000 controls and seed 20261020. Each cohort was scored in its complete global background with one reusable control cache [@Zhang2022scDRS].

The complete 47-group family covered all frozen lineages and 15 fibroblast states. Group tests compared the observed 95th score percentile with the corresponding control quantiles, using greater-than-or-equal ties, minimum P=1/1,001 and one BH47 adjustment. Patient contrasts were mean state score minus mean otherFB score after pooling captures within patient. Missing states remained NA. These descriptions did not treat cells, captures or algorithmic seeds as independent patient replicates.

### S4. Exclusions and external RNA transport

Three separate requests removed 20 direct ECM genes, 92 chromosome-1 genes or 61 genes within the published-window union, leaving 980, 908 or 939 members. Each request used the same official scoring settings and 1,000 newly matched controls. All 45 state-by-sensitivity tests formed BH45, separately from primary BH47. A descriptive residual analysis retained fixed technical/capture terms and ECM, TGFβ and cell-cycle proxies excluding direct genetic-set membership.

External GSE163973 retained 41,449 singlets, including 12,249 fibroblasts from three keloid and three ordinary-scar samples. Two discovery-reference classifiers used frozen 322- and 238-feature panels excluding genetic members and candidates, C=0.5, patient/class balancing and acceptance probability ≥0.5. Consensus required both references to accept FB01. Whole-cohort scDRS used the original gene request; all-FB and consensus-FB01 tests formed BH2. Prior use of this resource and shared genetic input make this expression-context transport rather than unseen genetic replication [@Deng2021fibroblast]. Sparse ordinary-scar consensus groups remained visible.

### S5. Candidate expression and regulatory predictions

All nine unique primary PP4≥0.8 candidates were evaluated without RNA-direction filtering. Patient/state pseudobulk CP10K, mean cell CP10K and detection fractions were recorded separately. CollecTRI/decoupler ULM and MLM used patient pseudobulk log2(CP10K+0.1) contrasts and at least ten measurable targets. Complete TF families were retained for full targets and fixed exclusion views removing genetic, candidate, mapping-panel and then direct-ECM features. Official ULM q values were retained; MLM P values received within-patient/view BH correction [@MullerDott2023CollecTRI; @Badia2022decoupler].

Classical and heat-network predictions used the same 1,353-gene WT networks: four cohort/context backgrounds, 122 cells per patient and three seeds. Their unsigned program-distance ranks were not interpreted as expression changes. Geneformer V2-104M used fixed cell memberships, official full-library rank encoding, natural-token deletion and a position-preserving sensitivity. With LODO state centers, S=(u_after−u_before)·(a_other−a_FB01); positive S denotes movement along that reference axis. Raw scores and post hoc nearby-token mean/median centering were retained. Backgrounds requiring four patients and at least three visible cells per patient remained NA when unsupported. Normal-anchor readouts used all 2,152 D0 fibroblasts with equal donor weighting. Network, embedding and expression endpoints were interpreted separately [@Osorio2022Knk; @Theodoris2023Geneformer].

### S6. Fixed programs in normal repair and public treatments

The shared 25-gene program excluded original genetic members and all nine candidates before treatment scoring. The original MAGMA1000 Z-weighted RNA proxy, ECM321 and CellCycle689 were fixed comparators. Sample scores used within-background average-tie gene ranks; assay-missing members were not replaced. Normal-repair profiles comprised three GSE241132 donors at D0/D1/D7/D30. Scores used 26,158 approved genes and all 25 marker members or 983 measurable MAGMA members. Donor differences were descriptive, not scDRS, PRS or FB01 abundance estimates [@Liu2025spatiotemporal].

Culture replicates were combined within biological source. GSE232435 supplied three primary foreskin sources under untreated, TGFβ and HS276-plus-TGFβ conditions. GSE232079 supplied paired Corin/DMSO cultures from four keloid sources; GSE7890 supplied paired hydrocortisone conditions in five keloid and four ordinary-scar strains. Counts used paired TMM–voom–limma models; processed arrays were log2-transformed once and modeled with paired limma. The seven contrasts retained full gene results. CAMERA/FRY used separate BH14 families for the two main and two comparator programs, with joint BH28 also reported; nine candidates by seven contrasts formed BH63. Fifteen native-state comparator panels were deterministically matched to baseline expression after excluding genetic/candidate/housekeeping members. Their post-discovery comparisons were descriptive [@Bale2023TAK1; @Almier2025Corin; @Smith2008HC; @Law2014voom; @Ritchie2015limma; @Wu2012CAMERA].

### S7. Paired response programs and ligand compatibility

Known-patient FB01/otherFB profiles required at least ten cells per group. Paired TMM–voom–limma with robust eBayes generated full-gene results. Signed top-250 lists required concordant moderated-t and fold-change directions after view admission. Four fixed views were full, non-definition, non-definition/no-ECM and non-definition/no-cell-cycle; the last two were parallel. Main and LODO lists were retained with their original whole-gene BH values.

NicheNet used the fixed 33,354-target by 1,226-ligand prior. Receiver backgrounds required ≥10% detection in at least one eligible FB01 profile. All ligands remained in all 144 calls, including Up/Down, main, LODO and patient views. Same-patient source/receiver support required at least ten cells and ≥10% detection, followed by a LIANA receptor-complex subunit audit. Canonical TGFBR1/TGFBR2 support was not replaced by alternative entries, and IGF2R was distinguished from mitogenic IGF1R. A known-TGFβ-stimulus culture comparison supplied technical calibration. Ligand ranking and coexpression were not patient P values, spatial proximity or receptor activation [@Browaeys2020NicheNet].

### S8. CellOracle models and matched readouts

CellOracle 0.20.0 used the official promoter prior and one cohort-specific fit. Fixed sampling retained at most 50 cells per patient–state, yielding 1,426 and 1,024 cells. The final 2,488-gene axis included all four fixed response families; this was set before study simulation. PCA used at most 30 components; balanced KNN used k=max(2,int(0.025N)), b_sight=8k and b_maxl=4k. State-specific bagging ridge used alpha=10, 20 bags and filtering at P=0.001/top 2,000 absolute-coefficient links. Global seed 20261021 and official internal seed 123 were fixed.

Each fit had empty, JUN KO, FOSL2 KO and one shuffled-GRN condition for each TF. TF expression was set to zero for three propagation steps with the official non-negative floor. Seven fixed programs were summarized on raw log1p(CP10K) and WT-SD scales, both with and without the perturbed TF. All states, patient slots, zero/NA and OOD diagnostics were retained; otherFB was an equal-state mean within patient. Patient predictions share the cohort fit. A single shuffle with different OOD behavior was descriptive, not an empirical-null test. Complete implementation, environment notes and parameter records accompany the analysis sources in the study GitHub repository [@Kamimoto2023CellOracle].
